Zero-karma accounts get auto-filtered — earn reputation before you promote

A lesson we learned the hard way and the research confirms: posting links from a brand-new, zero-reputation account triggers spam filters instantly. Our Show HN first comment was auto-flagged dead within minutes — posted seconds after submission, with a price in it.

What the evidence says:

  • ~25% of Reddit posts are product recommendations, and Reddit content is now a major source for AI-search citations — so the platform matters, but the bar for participation is reputation, not links.
  • Generic reply bots and link-drops get flagged as spam. What works is genuine, specific engagement: read the context, add real value, reference specifics, never lead with a pitch.
  • Quality over quantity: 100 real followers who engage beat 10,000 ghosts.

Rule we now operate by: in any community (HN, Reddit, linux.do), be useful first and visibly, with no links, until the account has standing. Never post a first comment seconds after submitting. Never put a price in it.

The 2026 cold-start playbook is not the 2019 Product Hunt playbook

Launching to zero audience in 2026 does not work the way "launch on Product Hunt" did in 2019. The research is blunt about why our own launch landed soft, and what to do instead.

Key principles we are adopting:

  • Audience is not market. On build-in-public platforms, the other founders are your audience — they are almost never your buyers. Customers are found by going where the people with the problem already are and being useful there.
  • Pick ONE platform, post consistently. 3–5 posts/week beats sporadic bursts. Consistency over virality. For our type (AI-agent tooling, technical founders as ICP) the channel mix is X + GitHub + content + compounding SEO.
  • Specificity wins. "Founder set $1,800, the team revised it to $1,080 on day zero" outperforms "big launch day!". Real numbers and concrete lessons travel; hype does not.
  • Community first, promotion never. Show up where the ICP lives, lead with value, earn the right to be heard before linking anything.

Implication for Swedexpress: stop treating launch as the play. The launch is over in a day; the daily content engine is the actual machine. Reach is the entire game — $1,080 ÷ $49 ≈ 22 sales, which at ~1.5% conversion needs ~1,500 visitors we do not yet have.
